shipped application — all 3D geometry, textures, icons, the logo, the PWA
icons, and the visual design — is **original work generated procedurally from
source in this repository**.

GHSA-mh99-v99m-4gvg, a high-severity ReDoS advisory reachable only through
build-time tooling. `pnpm audit` reports no known vulnerabilities.
